Decision Memory & Contradiction Detection

Unlike basic transcript summarizers, Meeting Mind remembers every decision across all past meetings. When a new meeting makes a contradictory decision, the system detects the clash, alerts the team, and allows you to formally resolve which decision should be active or superseded in corporate memory.
